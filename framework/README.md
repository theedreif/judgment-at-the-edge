# SIGNAL → CONDITION → LIMIT → DECISION → EVIDENCE

Judgment at the Edge is organized around five questions.

## SIGNAL — What changed?
Identify the cue, anomaly, event, or information that deserves attention.

## CONDITION — What is true now?
Interpret the current environment. A signal gains meaning from its context.

## LIMIT — What constrains the decision?
Identify boundaries including time, authority, policy, safety, system capability, resources, uncertainty, and mission requirements.

## DECISION — What action is justified?
Select an action appropriate to the signal, conditions, and limits.

## EVIDENCE — What makes the decision defensible?
Capture the observations, reasoning, action, result, and learning.

The loop turns information into observable judgment rather than treating knowledge recall as the final measure of capability.
