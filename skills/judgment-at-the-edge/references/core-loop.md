# Core Loop

## SIGNAL → CONDITION → LIMIT → DECISION → EVIDENCE

### SIGNAL
A cue, anomaly, event, contradiction, or new piece of information that deserves attention.

Questions:
- What changed?
- What was noticed?
- What should have been noticed?
- Is the signal reliable?
- What might be noise?

### CONDITION
The interpreted state of the operating environment.

Questions:
- What is true now?
- What does the signal mean in context?
- Has the operating state changed?
- What remains uncertain?

### LIMIT
A boundary on permissible or effective action.

Possible limits:
- authority;
- policy;
- safety;
- time;
- system capability;
- resources;
- uncertainty;
- mission requirements;
- communication;
- human workload.

Questions:
- What cannot be exceeded?
- What requires escalation?
- Which limit is known and which must be verified?

### DECISION
The action justified by the signal, condition, and limits.

Questions:
- What action is justified now?
- What alternatives exist?
- What is reversible?
- What would trigger a different action?

### EVIDENCE
The observable record supporting review and learning.

Capture where appropriate:
- signal observed;
- information available;
- assumptions;
- condition assessment;
- limits considered;
- decision;
- rationale;
- timing;
- communication;
- intervention or escalation;
- result;
- uncertainty;
- lesson.

## Principle

**READINESS BEFORE THE MOMENT → JUDGMENT IN THE MOMENT → EVIDENCE AFTER THE MOMENT**
