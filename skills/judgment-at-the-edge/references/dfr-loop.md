# DFR Decision Loop

## NOTICE → INTEGRATE → PRIORITIZE → DECIDE → DEFEND

Designed for Drone as First Responder and other operational environments where a human must synthesize multiple information sources under time pressure.

### NOTICE
Detect the cue that matters.

Avoid treating every alert as equally important. Identify anomalies, contradictions, changes, and mission-relevant signals.

### INTEGRATE
Combine relevant sources rather than making a decision from one feed in isolation.

Possible inputs can include video, telemetry, dispatch information, maps, weather, system status, communications, policy, and human reports. Use only sources actually available in the scenario.

### PRIORITIZE
Determine what matters now.

Consider consequence, urgency, uncertainty, mission objective, safety, authority, and resource constraints.

### DECIDE
Select justified action.

The decision should reflect the information available at the time and remain inside known limits.

### DEFEND
Make the reasoning reviewable.

Record what was noticed, what information was integrated, why priorities were set, what decision followed, and what evidence supports it.

## Human-machine teaming

The operator may need to:
- trust;
- verify;
- question;
- intervene;
- escalate;
- stop.

Do not assume automation confidence equals correctness.

## Design goal

Develop an operator who can **recognize, decide, act, explain, and update**.
